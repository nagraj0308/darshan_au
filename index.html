About Darshan

Darshan is a simple, fast, and beautifully designed application that helps you save, organize, and instantly access your favorite websites from one convenient place.

Whether you frequently visit shopping sites, educational portals, social media platforms, news websites, developer tools, or work resources, Darshan keeps everything organized and easily accessible.

Instead of searching through browser bookmarks or remembering long website addresses, simply open Darshan and access all your important websites with a single tap.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make web browsing simpler, faster, and more organized by providing an elegant platform where users can keep all their important website links together.

We believe productivity begins with quick access to the tools and websites you use every day.

Features

  • Save unlimited website links.
  • Create custom categories.
  • Quickly search your saved websites.
  • One-tap access to your favourite sites.
  • Organize work, shopping, education, news, and personal websites.
  • Simple, clean and easy-to-use interface.
  • Lightweight and fast performance.

Why Choose Darshan?

Darshan is designed for users who want a clean and efficient way to manage their favourite websites. With an intuitive interface and smart organization, you spend less time searching and more time getting things done.

Whether you're a student, professional, developer, or casual internet user, Darshan helps keep your online world organised.
